1. Who we are

TAPX ("the game", "we", "us") is a free tapping game. This policy explains what information the game stores, why, and what you can do about it. Contact: support email configured by the site owner.

2. What the game stores

The game stores only what it needs to run:

  • Account data: your email address, a username and an optional display name. These are managed by Supabase Auth and stored in Supabase's database.
  • Game data: total taps, coin balance, level, experience, highest combo, purchased upgrades, claimed milestones and your daily-reward streak.
  • Preferences: sound on/off and vibration on/off. These are stored against your account and also cached in your browser's local storage.
  • Sign-in security data: Supabase records sign-in timestamps and sessions so that your account stays secure.

We do not ask for your name, your address, your phone number, your payment details, your government ID, or your location. We do not build advertising profiles of you.

3. What is stored in your browser

A small amount of UI preference data is kept in your browser's localStorage: whether sound and vibration are enabled, the last username you typed, and an onboarding flag. This data never contains balances, sessions or anything you would mind losing, and you can clear it at any time with Settings → Reset local preferences.

Your Supabase session token is stored by the Supabase client so you stay signed in. That is standard for any web app that keeps you logged in.

4. Advertising

This site may display advertising in clearly marked, visible areas. The ads are served by a third-party advertising network (for example Adsterra) using that network's own code, and that network's own privacy policy governs what it collects.

The game itself:

  • does not hide advertising, hide it behind other content, or make it invisible;
  • does not load, refresh or request an advertisement when you tap;
  • does not simulate impressions, clicks or any other ad activity;
  • does not calculate or display advertising revenue anywhere in the game.

When an ad is served, that network may set its own cookies or use its own local storage. You can opt out through the network's own options, and your browser's privacy settings also apply. See the network's privacy policy for details.

5. Cookies

The game itself does not set advertising cookies. Cookies and local storage may be set by Supabase (to keep you signed in) and by the advertising network (to serve and measure ads). You can clear or block them in your browser settings; blocking them will stop you from staying signed in.

6. Who can see your data

  • Your own data: only you can read your profile, balance, upgrades and milestones. This is enforced in the database, not just in the interface.
  • Administrators: the game's administrators can see your username, masked email address, total taps, coin balance, level, join date and last-seen time. They cannot edit your balance except through the explicit, logged "coin adjustment" action.
  • Nobody else. Game data is never sold, rented or shared with advertisers.

7. How long data is kept

Your account and game data are kept for as long as your account exists. If you want your account and data deleted, contact the support address above. Deleting your account deletes your profile, game data, upgrades, milestones and daily-reward record.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. You can change your username, display name and preferences yourself in the game. For anything else, email the support address and we will respond.

9. Security

Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are never stored by this game. All access to game data is enforced by database Row Level Security, and every change to a balance, level or purchase is validated on the server. No system is perfectly secure, but we do not collect the personal data that would make a breach more damaging.

10. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be announced in the game.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy: support email. Advertising questions are answered by the network's own publisher support, not by this game.
